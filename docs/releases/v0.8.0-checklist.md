## Native browser checks — maintainer verified October 12, 2026

The checklist supplied for maintainer testing was: open the checkout's `index.html` in Chrome, Firefox and Safari. Use a separate test profile or temporary browser session when making test decisions, to keep your existing analyzer decisions separate. If storage is unavailable in that session, confirm that the persistence warning appears rather than treating that as saved-history verification.

- Import the October 2 complete CSV. Confirm the desktop layout is readable and controls do not overlap at the usual window size and at 125% zoom.
- Navigate forward and backward through pages, including the final page. Filter by artist and search a track in Missing Albums and Compilations; verify only matching rows show and counts update. Clear filters and switch Active / Fixed / Ignored views.
- Use Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter and Space to reach/operate upload, category controls, details, paging and decisions. Confirm focus is visible and remains sensible after acting on a card or its final row.
- Open details, type an unsaved correction choice, change page/filter and return. Confirm the draft and details survive. Use an album suggestion; confirm it fills the field without immediately saving a decision.
- In a test profile with storage available, mark one variation Fixed with a remembered choice and one per-track finding Ignored. Reload the same CSV; inspect Fixed / Ignored and Restore. Confirm the choice remains after Restore. Then load the older September export and confirm review-only wording and disabled decision changes.
- Export a report with dismissed items included and excluded. Confirm remembered choices and status tags, and that report contents cover the whole library rather than only the visible page.
