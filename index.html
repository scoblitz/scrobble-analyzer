Analyze your Last.fm history for duplicate artists, album variations, and other data quality issues.

📁 How to Get Your Export File

  1. Go to lastfmstats.com
  2. Enter your Last.fm username and wait for data to load
  3. Click the Export button to download your CSV
  4. Drag the file to the upload box in the sidebar, or click the box to browse

🆕 What's New in v0.6.0

  • Dismissals survive new exports - Now tied to your Last.fm username instead of the uploaded file. Existing dismissals migrate automatically.
  • Character reveal - Invisible characters and stray spaces now show as labeled chips, so you can see exactly what's different
  • Track-level invisible detection - Tracks now get the same invisible-character scan as artists and albums
  • One issue, one category - Variations that differ only by invisible characters now appear only under Invisible Characters
  • Export options - Choose whether reports include dismissed items
  • Fixes & polish - Titles with quotes parse correctly, "Gold" compilations are detected again, plus search/filter clear buttons and a calmer look
